## Audit Finding Bank

Use 3-5 only.

- Important service/product pages are missing strong SEO titles or meta descriptions.
- Quote/contact buttons are not visible enough on product/service pages.
- Product/service pages need clearer structure for Google and buyers.
- The site does not show enough proof: projects, certifications, reviews, testimonials, or technical documentation.
- Google review or testimonial collection is not automated.
- Website speed or mobile usability may reduce conversions.
- There is no clear follow-up system after a lead submits an inquiry.
- The site could use downloadable product catalogs or spec sheets.
- No visible CRM/dashboard flow for tracking inquiries.
- Industrial keywords are not mapped to dedicated pages.

## Recommended First Upgrade Options

Pick one:

- Rebuild the homepage and main service pages around quote conversion.
- Add product/service catalog pages with SEO and quote CTAs.
- Add a quote request workflow with automated follow-up.
- Add review/testimonial collection and trust signals.
- Add a dashboard to track inquiries, tasks, and monthly progress.
- Run technical SEO fixes first, then build content pages.
