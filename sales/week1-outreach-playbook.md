# Week 1 Outreach Playbook

Use this after the app deployment is live and the audit tool is available on `app.bizautomatrix.com`.

## Offer

Free Industrial Website + SEO + Automation Audit.

Positioning:

> We help engineering, manufacturing, railway supplier, and industrial automation businesses turn old websites into sales systems with SEO, product catalogs, quote requests, review automation, CRM dashboards, and AI-assisted workflows.

## Daily Target

- 10 new prospects researched.
- 5 personalized outreach messages sent.
- 2 follow-ups sent.
- 1 audit prepared for any interested reply.

## Qualification Checklist

Prioritize companies with at least two of these:

- Product or service catalog exists but is hard to search.
- Quote request or contact flow is weak.
- Website has poor SEO structure or thin service pages.
- No visible review/testimonial flow.
- Industrial, engineering, manufacturing, or railway buyer audience.
- Bangladesh export/supplier opportunity or USA local/industrial service opportunity.

## First Call Agenda

1. Confirm what they sell and who they sell to.
2. Ask where inquiries come from today.
3. Ask what happens after a quote request.
4. Show 3 audit findings.
5. Recommend one starter upgrade.
6. Offer paid next step.
