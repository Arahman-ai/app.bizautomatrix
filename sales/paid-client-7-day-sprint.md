## 15-Minute Call Script

Opening:

```text
Thanks for taking the time. I will keep this simple: I checked the website, found a few places where buyers may be dropping off, and I will show the first upgrade I would do if this were my business.
```

Ask:

- Where do most inquiries come from now?
- What happens after someone submits a quote/contact request?
- Which products or services bring the best profit?
- Do you track lost inquiries or follow-ups?
- Who updates the website now?

Show:

- 3 audit findings.
- 1 quote/inquiry improvement.
- 1 SEO improvement.
- 1 automation improvement.

Close:

```text
The first paid step I recommend is the Industrial Growth Starter. I can fix the highest-impact website, SEO, review, and inquiry-flow items first, then give you a monthly dashboard/reporting workflow.

For this first project, the starting investment is {{Price}}. If we start this week, I can deliver the first version in 7 days.
```

## Objection Responses

No budget:

```text
That makes sense. We can start small with only the highest-impact fixes: quote flow, SEO basics, and review capture. That keeps the first step low-risk.
```

Need to think:

```text
Of course. What would you need to see to feel confident this is worth doing first?
```

Already have a website person:

```text
That is good. I do not need to replace them. I can provide the audit, automation workflow, SEO task list, and implementation plan. Your current person can help with parts if needed.
```

Send proposal:

```text
Sure. I will send a short proposal with the first 7-day scope, deliverables, timeline, and price.
```

## Win Condition

A paid client is won when:

- They agree to the first scope.
- They pay a setup deposit or full starter fee.
- They provide website/admin access or request BizAutomatrix to guide their team.

Do not do full implementation before payment.
