## Day 2: Problem Post

```text
An old website quietly costs an industrial business money.

Not because it looks old.

Because buyers cannot quickly answer:

1. Do they sell what I need?
2. Can I trust them?
3. Have they done similar work?
4. Can I request a quote easily?
5. Will someone follow up?

For manufacturers, railway suppliers, engineering firms, and automation companies, the website should work like a sales assistant.

Product pages. Quote forms. SEO. Reviews. Follow-up. Reporting.

That is the upgrade we are focused on.
```

## Day 3: Educational Post

```text
Simple 5-point audit for any manufacturing website:

1. Can Google understand every product/service page?
2. Is there a clear quote request button on every important page?
3. Are project photos, certifications, and proof easy to find?
4. Does the site collect reviews or testimonials?
5. Does every inquiry enter a follow-up system?

If the answer is no, the website is not finished.

It may be online, but it is not yet working.
```
